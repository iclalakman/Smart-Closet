# Smart-Closet
A smart wardrobe assistant that solves daily outfit and color combination dilemmas based on weather conditions and styling modes.  
I realized that whenever I was about to go out, I spent way too much time struggling with what to wear and how to match colors correctly.
It’s a web/mobile-friendly application designed to automate and simplify daily outfit coordination.
The app filters your clothing options intelligently by adapting to 4 different weather conditions and 
features a custom "Pop Color Mode" for those who love bold, high-contrast color combinations.
